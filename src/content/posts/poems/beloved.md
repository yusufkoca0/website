What sin breaks me to kneel <br>
And leaves a wound not to heal? <br>
What weapon strikes me down <br>
In the burning heat of the dawn?

What leaves these eyes <br>
In darkness blind and unwise? <br>
Which tip pierces through pride, <br>
Tramples, mocks and casts aside?

Who dares to sing a lyre <br>
To deceive, enchant and aspire? <br>
What purpose is it strung  <br>
But to seal the breath within the lung?

Perhaps a figurine, <br>
Delicately obscene. <br> 
Carved from marble, dressed in grace, <br> 
With neither mercy nor remorse on her face.

What Goddess do you imitate <br>
And whose heart to intimidate? <br>
How much shame you wear <br>
As a medal of honor unaware?

What stains those sacred hands
And the truth your tongue bends?
What divine imagery you project
In an attempt to deny and reject?

What altar asks for such price, <br>
A heart for every sacrifice? <br>
What prayers have been left unsaid <br>
To keep your halo overhead?

Who are you and who am I? <br>
What reason to love and admire? <br>
How do all lose their meaning? <br>
Why wisdom is merely grieving?

Why does Heaven lend its brightest flame <br>
To those who never bear its name? <br>
Why does Heaven open its arms to embrace <br>
To those who never devote and never show grace?

Doomed shall I be then, <br>
Fall to my demise from Heaven. <br>
And a plea, turn back and look at me  <br>
Before you forget what I used to be.
